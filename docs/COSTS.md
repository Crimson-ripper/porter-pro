## Ways to keep it lower

- Use the **Claude Pro** plan in early phases; upgrade to Max only when you are coding daily for long sessions.
- Launch **Android only** first; skip Apple's ₹8,300 until iOS demand appears.
- Stay on free tiers until real traffic pushes you off them — the stack was chosen so that moving up is just a plan change.
- Apply for startup credits: AWS Activate, Google for Startups, Microsoft for Startups, Razorpay Rize, Startup India (DPIIT) recognition.
